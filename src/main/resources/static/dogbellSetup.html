Dogbell Setup

Register your Raspberry Pi doorbell and get text alerts.

How It Works

1Enter your Pi ID
2Add phone numbers
3Register & test

When your dog presses the doorbell button on the Pi, it sends a signal through MQTT. The server receives it and texts everyone you've added.

Register Your Pi

Enter your Pi ID, then add phone numbers for everyone who should get doorbell alerts.

Phone Numbers

Gateway depends on carrier: @tmomail.net (T-Mobile), @txt.att.net (AT&T), @vtext.com (Verizon), @messaging.sprintpcs.com (Sprint)
