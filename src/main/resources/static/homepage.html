Meet the Dogbell

A smart doorbell — for your dog.

The Dogbell is a Raspberry Pi–powered doorbell built for the comfort of your whole family, including your four-legged family members. Mount it by the door, and the when the Dogbell is pressed, you get an instant text message — wherever you are. No barks, no hassle.

It doesn't interfere with your existing doorbell, but provides a clear safe and dog friendly alternative. No extra app to install. Just a message sent to you and your family, with no obnoxious doorbell sound. Setup takes minutes, works with any cell phone, and is a must have for all dogbell owning families.
